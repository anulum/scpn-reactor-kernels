# Code of conduct

## Expected behaviour

Participants in this project act professionally and honestly:

- treat every participant with respect, regardless of background, identity,
  or experience;
- keep technical criticism specific, evidence-based, and directed at the
  work, not the person;
- report results truthfully — negative results, failed gates, and open
  problems are stated plainly, never hidden or inflated;
- respect the project's safety boundaries: no participant may present
  research output as operational, certified, or actuation-capable.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
publication of others' private information, and knowing misrepresentation of
scientific or engineering results.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to protoscience@anulum.li. Reports are handled by
the owner in confidence. Consequences scale from a private warning to
permanent exclusion from participation. Reporters acting in good faith will
not face retaliation.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces — the repository, reviews,
coordination channels, and any public communication made on the project's
behalf.
