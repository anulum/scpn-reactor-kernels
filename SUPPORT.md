# Support

The repository is local-first and has no public issue tracker yet.

- **Questions and technical contact:** protoscience@anulum.li
- **Security reports:** see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) — do not use public
  channels for vulnerabilities.
- **Commercial licensing:** protoscience@anulum.li (see
  [`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md)).
- **Project information:** <https://www.anulum.li>

When public hosting is established under separate owner authority, issue
forms under `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` become the reporting surface for bugs
and feature requests.
